Help & Details

Melbourne Delivery & Frequently Asked Questions

We offer same-day delivery across Melbourne for all orders received by 1:00 PM (AEST) Monday through Saturday. Orders received after 1:00 PM will be delivered on the next scheduled business day or on your selected future date.

We deliver throughout Greater Melbourne, including Melbourne CBD, South Yarra, Toorak, Richmond, St Kilda, Prahran, Carlton, Fitzroy, Brunswick, Footscray, Docklands, Hawthorn, Kew, Camberwell, Brighton, Doncaster, and surrounding metro suburbs. If your suburb is outside metro Melbourne, simply leave a note in your inquiry!

When you click "Buy Now" on any bouquet, you're taken to our simple Melbourne order form. Upon clicking "Send Order Inquiry", your mail client will open with all details neatly formatted. Once sent, we confirm receipt, verify flower availability for your requested date, and arrange your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, PayID, or Card Invoice).

Yes, absolutely! We curate custom designer hampers featuring Dior, Burberry, YSL, Chanel, and Estée Lauder. You can specify any specific fragrance or skincare preferences in the "Special Requests" field of the order form, or DM us directly on Instagram at @labellebouquett.

All fresh floral bouquets travel with wet stem packs to maintain optimum hydration. Hatboxes and baskets include specialized oasis floral foam infused with flower food so your blooms remain vibrant for days after arrival.
